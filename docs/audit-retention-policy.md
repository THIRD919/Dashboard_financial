# Audit Retention Policy

Audit, workflow and security events are append-only and ordinary users cannot update/delete them. Admin is view-only through the application. Retain financial workflow, approvals, locks, corrections, adjustments/reversals, imports and permission changes for a proposed minimum of 10 years, or longer when University/Thai legal policy requires.

Final duration, archive medium, legal hold, approved exceptional maintenance and disposal authorization are **FACULTY CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** and **BLOCKS GO-LIVE**. Any approved purge must occur only after verified immutable archival, dual authorization and a retained purge audit event.
