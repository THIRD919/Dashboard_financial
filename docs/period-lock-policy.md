# Period and Scope Lock Policy

A lock applies to one reporting period and one reporting scope: either Faculty or a specific Unit. It does not silently lock unrelated Units.

After lock:

- existing official rows cannot be updated or deleted;
- ordinary manual and Excel-import base entries are rejected;
- a correction requires a Vice Dean correction request;
- only a referenced Adjustment or exact Reversal may be added;
- the correction completes the normal two-level approval and lock workflow.

Lock is a distinct, explicit action after final approval and displays an irreversible-action warning.
