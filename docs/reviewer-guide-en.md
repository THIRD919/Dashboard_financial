# Reviewer Guide

Sign in with a REVIEWER account and open **Review**. The Office reviewer sees only the permitted Unit; the Faculty Secretary sees all Units.

1. Select **Start Review**.
2. Verify revenue, expense, operating result, margin, liquidity, source, period, Unit, and submitter.
3. Choose **Review Approve**, optionally adding a comment, or **Return for Correction** with a required reason.

You cannot review your own submission, perform final approval, or lock a period.
